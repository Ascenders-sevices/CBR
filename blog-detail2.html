Where the Ocean Meets the Kitchen

The beauty of coastal cuisine lies in its freshness, simplicity, and bold flavors. At Coral Beach Resort, the kitchen draws inspiration from the sea and surrounding coastal culture, crafting dishes that highlight fresh ingredients, aromatic spices, and time-honored recipes.

Every plate reflects the essence of the coast - vibrant, flavorful, and comforting. Whether it’s a seafood feast, a traditional South Indian meal, or an international dish, each bite captures the spirit of seaside dining.

Fresh Seafood, Straight from the Coast

One of the biggest highlights at Coral Beach Resort is its fresh seafood selection. Guests can enjoy a variety of expertly prepared dishes made with the freshest catch, ensuring unmatched taste and quality.

Prepared by skilled chefs, these dishes balance authentic coastal flavors with modern culinary techniques, delivering a perfect blend of tradition and innovation.

Signature Coastal Dishes You Must Try

Coral Beach Resort takes pride in serving signature dishes that showcase the richness of coastal Indian cuisine. Each recipe is crafted to highlight local flavors while appealing to a wide range of tastes.

A Perfect Balance of Local and Global Flavors

While coastal cuisine takes center stage, Coral Beach Resort also offers a diverse multi-cuisine menu to cater to all preferences.

  • Indian classics and regional specialties
  • Continental favorites like pasta, grilled meats, and salads
  • Asian-inspired dishes with bold sauces and flavors
  • Vegetarian and vegan options crafted with creativity
  • Kid-friendly meals for family stays

Romantic Dinners and Beachside Dining

Candlelight Dinners by the Beach

Imagine enjoying a private candlelight dinner with the sound of waves in the background, soft lighting, and a beautifully curated menu. This romantic setup is perfect for:

  • Valentine’s Day celebrations
  • Anniversaries
  • Honeymoons
  • Special proposals

Open-Air & Poolside Dining

Guests can also savor their meals in scenic open-air spaces or poolside seating, adding a relaxing resort-style vibe to every dining moment.

Breakfast with a View: The Perfect Start to Your Day

Mornings at Coral Beach Resort begin with a lavish breakfast spread, offering a mix of traditional and continental options.

  • Freshly prepared dosas, idlis, and South Indian specialties
  • Omelets, pancakes, waffles, and baked goods
  • Fresh fruit, juices, and hot beverages
  • Healthy options like salads, cereals, and smoothies

Paired with a calming sea breeze and beautiful views, breakfast becomes a refreshing and energizing experience.

Chef’s Specials: Creativity on Every Plate

The culinary team at Coral Beach Resort is passionate about innovation and presentation. The Chef’s Specials section features unique, seasonal, and experimental dishes that surprise and delight guests.

Desserts That End on a Sweet Note

No meal is complete without dessert, and Coral Beach Resort offers a delightful selection of sweet treats to complete your dining experience. Perfect for sharing with loved ones or indulging solo, these desserts add a memorable finish to every meal.

Perfect for Families, Couples, and Groups

For Families

Enjoy buffet spreads, kid-friendly dishes, and casual dining options suitable for all ages.

For Couples

Romantic dining setups, candlelight dinners, and intimate table arrangements create unforgettable moments.

For Corporate & Group Events

Customized menus, banquet catering, and premium service make Coral an ideal choice for meetings, celebrations, and corporate gatherings.

Hygiene, Quality, and Exceptional Service

At Coral Beach Resort, food safety, hygiene, and quality are top priorities. The kitchen follows strict cleanliness standards, ensuring every dish is prepared in a safe and hygienic environment.
